# Policies: Client A

Simulated: Client A and its rules are fictional.

## Managers

- Managers answer chats daily from 9:00 to 19:00.

## Viewings

- A manager arranges every viewing, daily from 10:00 to 19:00.
- The buyer is shown the flat type they ask about, in the complex they choose.

## Booking

- A manager books a flat, usually after a viewing.
- The agent never books a flat or takes a payment.

## What the agent never promises

- Discounts or special prices: only a manager can talk about them.
- Completion or move-in dates.
- A price, area or flat type that is not in the price list.
- That a specific flat, floor or view is still free.
- Mortgage approval or a mortgage rate.
